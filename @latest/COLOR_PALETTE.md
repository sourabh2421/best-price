# New Color Palette - Bold Modern (Option 3)

## 🎨 Color Scheme Applied

### Primary Color: Royal Blue
- **Hex**: `#2563EB`
- **Usage**: Main CTAs, primary buttons, headings, navbar active states
- **Shades**: 50-900 (from `#EFF6FF` to `#1E3A8A`)

### Accent Color: Sunset Orange  
- **Hex**: `#F97316`
- **Usage**: Secondary highlights, hover states, accent elements, icons
- **Shades**: 50-900 (from `#FFF7ED` to `#7C2D12`)

### Success Color: Emerald Green
- **Hex**: `#10B981`
- **Usage**: Success states, ratings, positive feedback, bullet points
- **Shades**: 50-900 (from `#D1FAE5` to `#022C22`)

---

## 🌈 Gradient Combinations

1. **Hero Background**: Blue-50 → Orange-50 blend
2. **Animated Blobs**: Blue-300, Orange-300, Blue-200/Orange-200 mix
3. **Gradient Text**: Primary-600 → Accent-500 → Primary-600
4. **Button Gradients**: Primary-600 → Primary-500
5. **Icon Hover**: Primary-600 → Accent-500
6. **Progress Bar**: Primary-600 → Accent-500 → Primary-600
7. **Card Accent Bars**: Primary-500 → Accent-500 → Primary-500

---

## 💡 Design Philosophy

**Bold Modern** combines:
- **Royal Blue**: Trust, professionalism, technology
- **Sunset Orange**: Energy, enthusiasm, warmth
- **Emerald Green**: Success, growth, positive action

This palette creates:
✅ High contrast for readability
✅ Energetic yet trustworthy feel
✅ Perfect for tech retail/mobile accessories
✅ Stands out from competitors
✅ Modern and memorable
